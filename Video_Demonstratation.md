
# Video Demonstration – RTL-to-GDSII Training

## Overview

This video demonstrates the knowledge and practical experience I gained during my 10-day RTL-to-GDSII training.

Throughout this training, I learned about Verilog RTL design, simulation, synthesis, static timing analysis, floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and physical verification.

I also worked on the VSDBabySoC project and explored tools such as Icarus Verilog, GTKWave, Yosys, OpenSTA, OpenROAD, and Magic.

This video presents my learning journey, practical work, and understanding of the RTL-to-GDSII design flow.
